My flagship personal project is TheraMind, a dual-mode clinical LLM agent. I fine-tuned Llama 3.2-3B on 9,250 clinical dialogues using QLoRA, which raised ROUGE-L from 0.40 to 0.78 on my evaluation setup, then aligned it with DPO (beta 0.1) on 49 preference pairs derived from the model’s own earlier mistakes paired with reference responses. A single 3B model handles both patient intake and SOAP-note generation through a task-switch chat template, with a ChromaDB RAG layer, safety prompts, constrained decoding and prompt-injection tests keeping the two modes from leaking into each other. It runs as a Dockerized FastAPI service on Hugging Face Spaces. To be precise, TheraMind is a live demo, not a production medical product: I read the ROUGE-L gain as better reference alignment, not as proof of clinical suitability. You can try it at https://vkm47-theramind-ai.hf.space.

I’m also building LEDGER, a self-verifying RAG system over U.S. federal budget and energy reports.
